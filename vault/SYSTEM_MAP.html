Inside one trading cycle

This pipeline runs every 30 minutes on each watchlist ticker. Nothing trades unless it survives every stage.

1 Gather data

200 days of price bars (Polygon for stocks, Alpaca for crypto) · live VIX level · fresh news headlines (NewsAPI) · FinViz fundamentals · insider & congressional filings · options-flow snapshot.

▼

2 Engineer features math

30+ technical indicators per ticker: RSI, MACD, Bollinger, ATR, Stochastic RSI, ADX, IBS, moving-average crossovers, volatility regime, momentum.

▼

3 Model prediction learned

XGBoost classifier (retrained weekly on fresh data) predicts next-move direction. ADX regime detection switches between momentum and mean-reversion playbooks.

▼

4 Reward-agent refinement reinforcement

The Q-learning agent — whose values update from the realized P&L of its own past decisions — can adjust the signal; a moving-average crossover rule can veto or force it.

Young layer: <100 trades of experience so far, so it's one voice among nine, not the driver. Roadmap: PPO agent trained offline on full history.

▼

5 Nine-source confidence vote consensus

Every signal source votes with a weight (chart below). Weighted direction + score decide: <65% → skip · 65–79% → scaled position · ≥80% → full position.

▼

6 Risk gates rails

Dexter AI veto on buys · VIX > 25 risk-off · 12% drawdown kill switch · crypto capped at 25% of portfolio · max 4 concurrent positions · 1% risk per trade · no entries after 3:30 PM.

▼

7 Smart execution orders

Limit-first with 1s IOC, market fallback · bracket orders carry the stop & take-profit · fills monitored and logged with confidence + signal breakdown attached.

▼

8 Position management exits

Scaled profit-taking: sell 40% at +3%, 30% at +6%, final 30% rides a 3% trailing stop · hard stop-loss at −6% (crypto −15%) · enforced at the top of every cycle.

Where each AI sits

Claude judgment

  • Every cycle: scores news headlines per ticker → sentiment vote
  • 9:00 AM: rewrites the watchlist from the journal + per-ticker stats
  • 6:30 PM: writes the trading journal (observation only — can't touch config)
  • On demand: portfolio chat advisor in the dashboard

Grok second opinion

  • Independent bullish/bearish bias vote per ticker (30-min cache)
  • Backup sentiment scorer if Claude is unavailable
  • Thin today — gets little context. Phase 2 feeds it the bot's own evidence.

Dexter veto gate

  • Nightly bias file: ok / caution / avoid per ticker
  • "Avoid" blocks new buys (never blocks sells/exits)
  • Fails open if the file is >24h stale — logged, never silently enforced

Cowork supervisors oversight

  • 9:20 AM: is the bot up? did rotation run? watchlist sane?
  • 4:30 PM: full audit — cycles, trades, decision quality → dated report in the vault; falls back to the Alpaca API if the Mac is offline

How it learns

Weekly retrain statistical

  • XGBoost refits on the newest market data every week — the model's view of "what predicts an up-move" stays current.

Reward loop reinforcement

  • Every cycle, the Q-agent's action values update from the actual P&L its past choices produced. Exploration is off in live trading — it only exploits what it has learned.

Journal → rotation qualitative

  • Claude's nightly review of what worked and what didn't feeds the next morning's ticker selection. The slowest loop, and currently the smartest one.

Calibration milestone upcoming

  • At ~200 logged trades: fit confidence → real win probability (Platt scaling), then switch position sizing to fractional Kelly. Every trade now logs the data this needs.
