Confidence Scoring

Weighted vote · v1

The 8 sources don't agree, they vote. Each contributes a directional bias scaled by its weight, then the weights renormalize based on detected market regime (ADX-based).

Grok bias (secondary AI) 20%
Claude sentiment (news analysis) 15%
MA crossover (trend) 15%
IBS mean-reversion (oversold) 15%
Technical indicators (RSI/MACD/BB) 15%
ML prediction (XGBoost) 10%
FinViz fundamentals 5%
Insider + Congress filings 5%
Regime override: in trending markets (ADX>25), MA-crossover weight goes up. In choppy markets (ADX<15), IBS goes up. Wrong strategy on wrong regime is the #1 way trend systems fail in chop.

3-Tier Scaled Exits

Lock in profit early, let the runner ride. The position monitor checks every cycle and submits the partial sell when each gain threshold trips.

Hard stop-loss

Sits server-side at Alpaca. Fires regardless of which exit tier the position is in. Works even if the local position monitor crashes.

05 · Cost Model

Backtest Friction

Strategies must clear ~30 bps of round-trip drag before any "edge" is real. Slippage + transaction cost are configurable; defaults are a blended retail floor.

Unit-verified: 100 round-trips on $50 stock with $10K notional bleeds exactly $1,500.

Reality check:

· Liquid stocks (SPY/QQQ) — closer to 10–15 bps. Defaults over-penalize.

· Mid-caps — 20–30 bps. Defaults are about right.

· Crypto on Alpaca — 70–100 bps round-trip. Defaults under-penalize.

Per-asset-class slippage is on the homework list.

06 · Disclosure

Honest Limitations Read before trusting any backtest

Six known sources of bias. None are fully fixed yet. The bot is research software — paper trade for months and benchmark against a passive index before you trust it with live money.

01 Confidence is not a probability
▾
The 8-source score is a heuristic weighted vote, not a calibrated win probability. Kelly sizing was implemented, then disabled for this reason. Confidence is now logged on every trade — after ~200 trades, Platt scaling can map raw confidence → actual win rate, and Kelly can come back honestly.
02 Sentiment + fundamentals leak in backtests
▾
add_sentiment_features and add_fundamental_features fetch current values and broadcast them across every historical bar. Backtests now neutralize these features via the is_backtest=True flag. Live cycles are unaffected. Proper fix (point-in-time joins on publishedAt) is a future project.
03 PDT cutover gate
▾
FINRA Rule 4210 amendments take effect June 4, 2026. Both bot_service and Trader_main enforce a runtime gate: before that date, day_trading_enabled is forced to False regardless of config. After the date, config takes over automatically. Self-correcting.
04 Q-learning state space is undertrained
▾
The Q-table is a CSV with no atomic-write guarantees. With under 100 matched trades of history, RL hasn't learned anything statistically meaningful. Confidence + regime detection do the actual decision-making. Refactor (deep-Q net or drop entirely) is on the Tier 3 list.
05 Multi-AI consensus is correlated
▾
Claude and Grok are not independent voters. They train on overlapping web data and reasoning patterns. The 15% + 15% weighting in the confidence score overestimates the second opinion's true information content.
06 Hardcoded confidence weights
▾
The 8 weights in config.yaml → confidence are unjustified by data. They're a v1 starting point. Sensitivity sweep (±5% per weight) needed before treating them as load-bearing.
